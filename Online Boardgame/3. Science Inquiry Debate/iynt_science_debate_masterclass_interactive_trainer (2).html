Master the Science Fight 掌握三大競賽角色與攻防邏輯

Rule 1: Rotation

3-Team Fight Rotation (三隊輪替)

In Stage I, Team 1 reports, Team 2 opposes, Team 3 reviews. Teams rotate roles across Stage II and Stage III so every team acts in each role once.

Rule 2: Challenges

Rejections & NR Penalty (拒答扣分)

Teams can reject up to 3 problem challenges free of penalty. From the 4th rejection onward, the Reporter's grade multiplier $C$ is reduced ($0.9, 0.8, 0.7$).

Rule 3: Individual Quotas

Individual Role Limits (個人上限)

No student may present as Reporter, Opponent, or Reviewer more than twice during the entire selective Science Fights. Collaboration requires team distribution!

3. Scientific Inquiry Thinking & Methodology可探究思考模式與實驗評估

How Scientists Solve Open-Ended Problems (探究思考四步法)

IYNT problems are open-ended research tasks. Master the C-E-R framework, error isolation, and qualitative-to-quantitative transition models.

STEP 1

Qualitative Observation to Quantitative Parameterization

Do not just observe "it gets bigger". Convert qualitative phenomena into dimensionless parameters (e.g., Reynolds number $Re$, aspect ratio $L/D$, normalized intensity $I/I_0$).

STEP 2

Single-Variable Isolation Matrix (單一變數控制矩陣)

Ensure all secondary variables are strictly held constant. Use differential sensing or active cooling/heating loops to eliminate thermal drift and ambient humidity noise.

STEP 3

First-Principles Theoretical Modeling (第一性原理建模)

Derive a physical or chemical formula starting from conservation laws (mass, energy, momentum) or thermodynamic equilibria rather than fitting arbitrary polynomial curves.

STEP 4

Error Analysis & Boundary Limits (誤差分析與邊界極限)

Calculate systematic vs random errors. Always display standard deviation error bars ($\pm \sigma$) and report $R^2$ goodness-of-fit alongside asymptotic physical limits.

The C-E-R Jury Response Framework (評審問答 C-E-R 答辯法)

C - Claim (主張) Give a direct, concise "Yes/No" or quantitative answer immediately without rambling.
E - Evidence (數據證據) Point directly to a specific slide or graph number: "As seen on Slide 14, Sensor Log B..."
R - Reasoning (理論推論) Tie the empirical data back to your governing equation or chemical equilibrium law.

5. Scientific Presentation Guidelines 科學簡報與發表準則

How to Structure a Winning 12-Minute Reporter Slide Deck (12分鐘主講簡報架構)

Jurors award high scores to clean, quantitative slides. Follow this standard 15-slide architecture designed for international tournaments.

Slides 1–3: Introduction

Title, Phenomena & Hook

  • Slide 1: Title, Team Name, Problem Statement
  • Slide 2: High-speed video / qualitative phenomenon
  • Slide 3: Research Questions & Parameters Defined
Slides 4–7: Setup & Theory

Experimental Rig & Equations

  • Slide 4: Experimental Apparatus Schematic + Photo
  • Slide 5: Sensor Calibration & Error Controls
  • Slide 6–7: Theoretical Derivation from First Principles
Slides 8–12: Data & Discussion

Theory vs Experiment Overlay

  • Slide 8–10: Data graphs with error bars & fitted curves
  • Slide 11: Non-dimensional parameterization
  • Slide 12: Discrepancies & Discrepancy Explanation

High-Scoring Slide Practices (加分項)

  • Label all graph axes with explicit units (e.g. $T$ [°C], $v$ [m/s]).
  • Include standard deviation error bars ($\pm \sigma$) on every data point.
  • Place the theoretical formula directly above or beside the curve plot.

Common Slide Pitfalls (扣分扣項)

  • Overcrowding slides with dense paragraphs of unread text.
  • Showing raw Excel spreadsheets instead of formatted vector plots.
  • Hiding apparatus photos or failing to show scale bars on micrographs.
