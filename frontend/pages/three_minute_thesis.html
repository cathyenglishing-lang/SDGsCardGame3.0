Level 1

What is 3MT?

Core idea: be clear, concise, engaging, and understandable to a non-specialist audience. Always follow the current rules of your institution or organizer.

⏱️Three Minutes
Communicate the problem, significance, research, and value within a very limited time.
🖼️One Visual Focus
Use one focused visual to support the story rather than filling the slide with text.
👥General Audience
Use language that people outside your field can understand. Avoid jargon overload.

Level 3

Script Builder：Build a Clear 3MT Story

Do not start by chasing perfect English. Build the logic first, then refine the language. A useful basic arc is Hook → Problem → What I Do → Why It Matters → Finisher.

Choose Your 3MT Structure （架構）

Your research does not have to follow one fixed pattern. Choose the structure that best matches the message you want the audience to remember.

Problem–Solution （問題－解決）Problem → urgency → research response → impact → conclusion
Narrative （敘事）Story → context → conflict → research resolution → reflection
Thematic （主題式）One central idea → 2–3 connected themes → synthesis
Comparative Analysis （比較分析）Option A vs. B → evidence → key difference → implication
Journey of Discovery （探索歷程）Question → search → turning point → discovery → meaning
Visual & Analytical （視覺分析）Start from one visual → observe → explain → reveal significance
Recommended flow: Hook → Problem → Research Response → Impact → Finisher.

Simulated 3MT Evaluation

This is a practice evaluator, not an official competition score. It gives simulated feedback based on your current script and slide. Use it to identify what to revise before rehearsal.

Six Evaluation Dimensions

Content （內容）
Is the research problem, purpose, finding, and significance understandable?
Organization （組織）
Does the talk move logically from hook to problem, research, impact, and conclusion?
Audience Engagement （觀眾投入）
Does the talk create curiosity, relevance, and a memorable story?
Language （語言）
Is the English clear, concise, accessible, and low in unnecessary jargon?
Delivery （表達）
Is the script likely to support confident pacing, emphasis, and oral delivery?
Slide Design （投影片設計）
Does one visual idea support the message without competing with the speaker?
