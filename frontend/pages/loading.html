Pecha Kucha Guide

What is PechaKucha?

PechaKucha (Japanese for "chit-chat") is a highly visual presentation format where you show exactly 20 slides for 20 seconds each, advancing automatically for a total of 6 minutes and 40 seconds.

The Core Concept: "20x20"

20 SlidesThe deck is strictly limited to 20 images.
20 SecondsEach slide stays on screen for exactly 20 seconds.
6:40 TotalThe entire presentation takes exactly 6 minutes and 40 seconds.

Key Characteristics

Automated Pacing: The timer is built into the software. You cannot pause, rewind, or skip ahead, forcing a relentless forward momentum.
Visual Dominance: Slides rely on powerful imagery rather than bullet points, charts, or text blocks.
Storytelling Focus: The format shifts the presentation away from a dry lecture and into a fluid, rehearsed narrative performance.

Why People Use It

Created by Tokyo architects in 2003, it was designed to force speakers to get straight to the point and prevent "Death by PowerPoint." It ensures that events move quickly, keeping audiences engaged across multiple topics in a single evening.
