Around the World
in 80 Conversations

Ten zones. Eight conversations in each zone. You do not win by owning the most — you win by connecting the widest.

Travellers (2–4)
How to play. Roll, move, and land on a zone. Draw a Conversation Card and say your answer out loud in English — the group decides if you made the connection. Succeed and you claim the space and earn Connection Points.

Landing on someone else's space? Answer their follow-up question instead of paying full toll.
Culture Shock! sends you to Lost in Translation. The only way out is to complete a Repair card — avoiding the problem never works.

Final bonuses: Zone Master +200 · Bridge Builder +300 (spaces in 5+ zones) · Repair Hero +150 (3+ repairs).
